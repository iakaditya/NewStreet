HOW IT WORKS

From problem to resolution.
Four simple steps.

AI assists classification and routing. Government departments remain responsible for action and resolution.

01

Tell Us

Citizen describes the issue in their language — text, voice, or photo.

02

AI Understands

AI classifies the grievance, identifies category, location, and relevant information.

03

Right Department

New Street routes the grievance to the appropriate government department automatically.

04

Track & Resolve

Citizen receives status updates until resolution and confirms the fix is complete.

TRANSPARENCY

Know what happens after you report.

You are never left wondering "Did anyone see my complaint?" Every step is visible to you.

Submitted
Your complaint is recorded with a unique ID and timestamp.
AI Classified
AI assigns category, priority level, and relevant department.
Department Assigned
Routed to the correct government department with an SLA deadline.
Officer Acknowledged
The assigned officer confirms receipt and schedules field visit.
Work In Progress
Field crew dispatched. Photo updates uploaded in real time.
Resolved
Issue fixed. Before/after images uploaded and geo-verified.
Citizen Feedback
You confirm and rate the resolution. Expenditure published publicly.
