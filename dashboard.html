Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a complaint to be assigned?

Complaints submitted on New Street are routed within 2 hours to the respective PWD, BESCOM, or BWSSB division engineer.

How do I track my property tax payment status?

Navigate to the Gov Services tab, click on Property Tax, and enter your 10-digit SAS Assessment Code.

What is Sahayak AI and how does it work?

Sahayak AI is your 24/7 civic assistant capable of checking scheme eligibility, translating documents, and lodging voice complaints.
