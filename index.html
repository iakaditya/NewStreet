STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION

How New Street Works
for Every Citizen in India.

A proven 5-step accountability pipeline engineered to eliminate red tape.

✓
SLA Guaranteed Field Dispatch

1

Snap Photo or Voice Note

Open the app, take a picture of the broken pipe, pothole, or garbage spot. The app automatically extracts high-precision GPS coordinates.

2

AI Analyzes & Categorizes

Sahayak AI verifies image authenticity, checks if an identical issue was already reported nearby, and assigns priority level P1/P2/P3.

3

Direct Municipal Assignment

Ticket lands directly on the field tablet of the designated Ward Junior Engineer with an automated countdown SLA clock.

4

On-Site Repair with Proof

Work crew arrives, completes the physical repair, and submits before/after timestamped photos verified via GPS bounds.

5

Citizen Verification & Rating

You receive an SMS/app alert to confirm the repair and rate the officer. The budget expenditure is logged publicly on the ward ledger.
